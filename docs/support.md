Email **[contact@papathemes.com](mailto:contact@papathemes.com)**. You can also use **Contact support** in the app
(on Overview, on Help, and on a model that couldn't be processed).

## What to include {#what-to-include}

So we can help on the first reply, please include:

- **Your store name** (and the storefront, if you have more than one).
- **The product or model name** the question is about.
- **What you did** and what you expected to happen, step by step.
- **Screenshots** of what you see. For a storefront problem, a screenshot of the
  [`?p3d-debug=1` overlay](/docs/storefronts/#installation-check) and the product page address.
- For a processing problem: the **error code**, the **file name**, and the text under **Technical detail** or
  **Details for support** on the model page. If you can, attach the file or a download link.
